Founding Athletes · Fall 2026

Train for your event. Help shape Justus.

We are choosing about 30 athletes with a real event on the calendar to use Justus free through their training block and help us improve the coach.

Apply for the cohort About three minutes · Applications reviewed on a rolling basis

Who it is for

A real goal, with real life around it.

You do not need to be elite. You do need a dated event and a willingness to tell us what the coach gets right or wrong.

  • An event in the coming monthsA marathon, half, triathlon, ride, swim, or another meaningful finish line.
  • A watch or training deviceWorkouts that sync through Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Brief, honest feedbackA short weekly check-in while you train.

What you receive

A private coach for the whole block.

Justus helps plan the week, reads what actually happened, and adjusts when training meets travel, fatigue, soreness, or missed sessions.

  • Free access through your eventNo card and no automatic conversion to a paid plan.
  • Direct contact with the founderYour feedback goes straight to Gideon and shapes the product.
  • Private by designNo feed, followers, leaderboards, or public training profile.

Tell us what you are training for.

The form asks about your event, your device, and how you plan training today. Every application is read by Gideon.

A few questions.

Is the program free?

Yes. Selected athletes use Justus free through their event. No card is required.

What events and devices are a fit?

Running, triathlon, cycling, swimming, and other dated events are welcome. Justus works on iOS and Android and reads workouts through Apple Health and Health Connect.

How much feedback is expected?

A short weekly check-in and honest notes when something works or does not. No long surveys or scheduled testing sessions.

What happens after my event?

Nothing converts automatically. Founding athletes keep free access through the research preview and can decide later whether they want to continue.
