Why we are doing this

A chatbot can give good advice. That doesn’t make it a coach.

The idea for Justus started when Gideon watched a marathon-running friend use an AI chatbot as a coach. The advice could be useful, but his friend still had to track workouts somewhere else, explain what happened, keep the plan up to date, and carry the context from one conversation to the next.

Gideon works in software in Boston and has been a runner for most of his life — not an elite one, just someone who wanted to understand his own training better. He started building Justus around a simple idea: AI intelligence is surprisingly good, but it needs the rest of the coaching system around it. A memory of who you are. Your actual workouts. A plan that can change. Proactive engagement.

Founding Athletes will help find out where that works in real training and where it still needs to get better.

Who we are looking for

A real goal, with real life around it.

We’re looking for people with an event on the calendar who will genuinely use Justus as their coach in the weeks leading up to it.

  • An event in the coming monthsA marathon, half, triathlon, ride, swim, or another meaningful finish line.
  • Serious use while you trainPlan with Justus, ask questions, log what happened, make changes, and keep using it when the original plan stops fitting the week.
  • Brief, honest feedbackA short weekly check-in. Tell us when the coach is useful, wrong, confusing, or missing something.

What you receive

A private coach through your event and a real say in what gets built.

Justus helps plan your training, reads what you actually did, remembers the context, and can reshape the weeks ahead when travel, fatigue, soreness, or a missed session changes the plan.

  • Free access through your eventNo card and no automatic conversion to a paid plan.
  • A direct line to GideonGideon is building Justus here in Boston. If the coach makes a bad call, something is confusing, or you have an idea that would make it more useful, your feedback reaches him directly and can shape what’s built next.
  • Private by designNo feed, followers, leaderboards, or public training profile.

A few questions.

Is the program free?

Yes. Selected athletes use Justus free through their event. No card is required.

What events and devices are a fit?

Running, triathlon, cycling, swimming, and other dated events are welcome. Justus works on iOS and Android and reads workouts through Apple Health and Health Connect. Any wearable that sends workout data to Apple Health or Health Connect can work with Justus; you just need to connect it there so the data can flow to Justus.

How much feedback is expected?

A short weekly check-in is the baseline. Beyond that, tell us when something works, when the coach gets something wrong, or when the product gets in your way.

What happens after my event?

Nothing converts automatically. Founding Athletes keep free access through the research preview and can decide later whether they want to continue.
