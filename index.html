Why Justus

A coach, not a dashboard — and not a stranger.

i.

vsThe data trackers

Garmin · Apple Health · Fitbit · Suunto · Coros · Polar

They measure beautifully. They don't plan.

Heart rate zones, splits, training load, sleep stages, recovery scores; the wearables ecosystem is extraordinary at telling you what happened. The few that try to plan (Garmin Coach, the new Google Health Coach) are tied to their own watches, built as features on top of a data dashboard, and run by companies whose business is your data. Justus is a coach first: the plan leads, the data supports, and your watch doesn't matter.

ii.

vsGeneral chatbots

Claude · ChatGPT · Gemini

Brilliant, but a stranger every time.

General-purpose AI is powerful, but you have to re-explain your situation, paste data, upload screenshots, and remember to come back. It can't see Tuesday's workout unless you tell it. It can't write the plan you log against, and it can't follow up. Justus holds a durable profile across years, maintains an actual plan, and reaches out when it should, not just when you remember to ask.

iii.

vsRunna

The running-first training app

Excellent for one race. Less so for the rest of your life.

Runna is the best running-first app on the market. Its plans are template-driven by elite coaches and adjust pace targets as you progress. But the strength work is supplementary, the adaptation is mostly pace-based, and it doesn't really know about your sleep, your travel week, or the lift you did yesterday. Justus is broader and conversational: tell it about a sore knee or a missed week and the plan reshapes around you, across every modality you train.

Full comparison: Justus vs Runna· vs TrainingPeaks

iv.

vsThe strength loggers

Fitbod · Strong · Hevy

Great progression. Stops at the gym door.

These have some of the best strength logging and progression in the category. They also can't see your sleep, your HRV, the run you did this morning, or whether you're heading into a deload. And they're strength-only by design, so if you also run, cycle, or swim, you're juggling apps. Justus programs strength alongside every other modality, and reads the recovery signals the loggers can't see.

Full comparisons: vs Fitbod· vs Strong· vs Hevy· vs Caliber

v.

vsThe recovery wearables

Whoop · Oura · Fitbit Air

A complement, not a competitor.

These read your body (sleep, HRV, readiness) better than anything. What they don't do is build or log workouts. Pair one with Justus and the recovery data flows into a coach that can act on it: lighten today's session, push tomorrow's intervals, hold the deload when your body is asking for it.

Truly AI-native. Quiet by design.

A conversation, not a form. One coach across every surface, with shared context. No gym-bro themes, no "perfect" bodies, no walls of unexplained numbers, no leaderboards, no feeds, no friend requests.

Frequently asked

A few things worth knowing.

What types of training are supported?

Running, strength, cycling, swimming, HIIT, mobility, yoga, rowing, triathlon, and hybrid combinations of any of these. Justus programs across modalities in a single coordinated plan. Your long run won't land the day after a heavy lower-body session unless you want it to.

Whether you're picking up weights for the first time or training for your 25th marathon, Justus is trained on the relevant literature.

Is my data really private?

Yes. No social features, no feeds, no leaderboards. Your training is between you and your coach. We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers. You can export or delete everything at any time. Just us.

Do I need a wearable or specific gear?

No. Justus works from a conversation. If you have an Apple Watch, Garmin, Whoop, Oura, or any device that talks to HealthKit or Health Connect, great. That data flows in and shapes your plan. If you don't, Justus will ask the right questions and plan from there.

When does the research preview start, and what does it cost?

We're opening access in small waves through 2026. Pricing isn't final, but the research preview will be low-cost for early users while we refine what a private AI coach should feel like.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

You get a personal referral link you can share. Referrals move you up the waitlist; the more friends you bring along, the sooner you get in.
