Welcome to the beta.

A small private group. Thank you for being one of the first.

I'm sharing a working version of Justus with a handful of people I trust. Your honest reactions are what will shape the product before it goes out into the world.

What I'm hoping from you

An honest read, the friction you notice, real usage.

  • The honest read. Would you actually use this? Is it helping? Would you pay for it, and what price would feel fair?
  • The friction you notice. Bugs, design weirdness, things you wished it did, things it did badly.
  • Real usage. The more you actually use it, the more data I have to evaluate how the coach is performing.
To get the most out of it

A few things will make the app work the way it's meant to.

  • Do the onboarding honestly. The goal is to land on a plan you actually want to try.
  • Connect your fitness data during onboarding so the coach can adapt to you.
  • Turn on notifications and connect your calendar so your workouts show up where you live.
Watch data sync during beta

Smooth for Apple Watch + iPhone. A workaround for everyone else.

If you're on an iPhone with an Apple Watch, your data flows in automatically from Apple Health once you connect it.

If you're on Android, or on an iPhone with any other watch (Garmin, Fitbit, Whoop, Coros, Polar, Suunto), the sync I've wired up for the beta isn't the integration that will ship in production. You will have a partial historical pull at best and less data in the ongoing sync. The app still works; the coach just won't see the full picture.

The workaround: install Health Sync and let it bridge your wearable into Apple Health or Google Health Connect. It's free for 30 days, or a one-time $4 if you want to use it longer and/or get a full historical data sync. I use it with my Garmin.

The paid integration that handles all wearables natively is on the roadmap; it just isn't wired up for the beta.

Privacy during the beta

A small note on what's different right now.

During the beta I can see your data and your app usage, and I'll probably know who you are. None of your data goes upstream to the AI providers; it stays on a private gateway I run for the beta.

After the beta, you move to the standard privacy posture described in our privacy policy.

Feedback and bugs

Three ways to tell me something's off.

  • Onboarding issues. Text me a screenshot.
  • Coach responses that feel off. Use the thumbs up or thumbs down right on the message.
  • Anything else. Use the feedback form on the settings page. Attach a screenshot if it helps.

Get the app

Install on iOS or Android.
