Delete Your Justus Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Justus Health account and associated app data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Justus Health account, email us from the email address associated with your Justus account.

Email: coachjonathanapp@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete my Justus account

Request deletion by email

We may ask you to verify that you control the account before completing the deletion request.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete your account and associated Justus app data, including:

  • Your account record and profile information
  • Your fitness goals, training preferences, schedule, equipment access, and injury history
  • Your coaching conversations and voice-input transcripts
  • Your training plans, workout logs, and activity history
  • Health and fitness data imported from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect

What may be retained

We may retain limited records where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or safety purposes. These retained records are limited to what is necessary, such as timestamps, account identifiers, security logs, or safety/compliance event records.

These retained records do not include your full coaching conversation history, training plans, workout logs, or imported health and fitness data.

Deletion timing

We aim to process verified deletion requests promptly. Account and app data deletion will be completed within 30 days unless a longer retention period is required by law or needed for security, fraud-prevention, or safety reasons.

Delete some data but keep your account

If you want to delete specific data while keeping your Justus account, email us with the details of the data you want deleted. We will review the request and respond with available options.

Privacy policy

For more information about how Justus collects, uses, and protects data, see the Justus Privacy & Data Use Policy.
